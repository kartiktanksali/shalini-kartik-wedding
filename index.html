Our Story

Turns out the best way to resolve a heated political debate is to marry the other side.
We are choosing to see this as diplomacy.

Blame the Algorithm

Every good Bay Area love story has a little algorithmic meddling in it — and ours is no exception. A very presumptuous recommendation, a city full of possibility, and two people who probably should have been working instead.

In Transit

Long distance traversing 8450 miles and 15 time zones sounds romantic until you're doing the time-zone arithmetic at 3am. One of us was always awake at the wrong hour. Another always about to land. We got very good at sounding awake when we weren't, at packing for two seasons in one carry-on, and at explaining to suspicious visa officers exactly why we kept doing this to ourselves.

Off Script

Like a low-budget action film, this story has far too many random locations, an incoherent plot, and a frankly absurd amount of whale chasing. Throw in a few active volcanoes, a mistaken international border crossing on a motorcycle, an eviction, and you have our timeline.
But when the plot went completely off-script as it often does in life, we realized there’s no one else we’d rather have co-directing the chaos.

And now

This September, beneath the towering redwoods at Ben Lomond, we're closing the last little distance for good. Our favorite stories have always had you in them, and we couldn't possibly start our best one yet without you.

Getting There

Up the mountain.

The Sequoia Retreat Center · 11445 Alba Road, Ben Lomond, CA 95005

About 45 minutes from San José. Two ways up: take Highway 17 south toward Santa Cruz for the quick route, or Highway 9 through the mountains if you'd rather have fun behind the wheel. Either way you'll wind through Ben Lomond, then up 1.6 miles of Alba Road to the top. (Yes, there's cell service up there. Mostly.)

Flying in

San José (SJC) is your best bet — about 50 minutes away and the easiest drive up. San Francisco (SFO) has more international options and sits about 1.5 hours out. Both have rental cars; you'll want wheels either way to reach the venue.


Come say hello

We're just over the hill at City Gate Apartments in Cupertino, and the days before the wedding will be full of the usual pre-wedding chaos — family, friends, food, and a fair amount of happy nonsense. If you're flying in early and want to be part of it, we'd genuinely love to have you.

Parking

Parking on the property is limited and the lots are spread out. We'd genuinely love it if you carpooled up the mountain — once you rsvp, we'll help match riders to spare seats (If it works!).

Stay & Explore

Make a weekend of it.

Ben Lomond and neighboring Felton are tucked deep in the redwoods, with a handful of welcoming places to stay just minutes from the venue — and plenty worth doing if you come up early or linger a day.

A place to rest your head

Brookdale Lodge

★ 4.3 (479) · 3-star · 0.7 mi from the venue

A storied 1920s redwood lodge with newly remodeled rooms, private balconies among the trees, and a 1950s-style cafe and bar on site. Pet-friendly. The closest bed to the wedding.
